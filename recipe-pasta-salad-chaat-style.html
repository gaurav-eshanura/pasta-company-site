Pasta Salad Chaat Style

Cold pasta dressed chaat-style with lemon, tamarind and a masala punch. Made for potlucks, picnics and the five minutes before guests arrive.

  • 20 minutes
  • Serves 4
  • Easy

Cook it with

Pasta Mix

Try & taste · Har shape ka maza ek saath.

All pasta

Ingredients

  • 250 gPasta Mix, cooked and cooled
  • 1Onion, finely chopped
  • 1Tomato, chopped
  • 1Cucumber, chopped
  • 1/2 cupSweet corn
  • 1 tbspLemon juice
  • 1 tspChaat masala
  • 1/2 tspRoasted cumin powder
  • 2 tbspCoriander leaves
  • 1 tbspTamarind chutney

Method

  1. Cook the pasta, drain, and rinse under cold water until completely cool. Drain again and spread out so it does not clump.
  2. Combine the onion, tomato, cucumber and corn in a large bowl.
  3. Add the cooled pasta, lemon juice, chaat masala, cumin, tamarind chutney and coriander.
  4. Toss well, taste for salt and lemon, and chill for 10 minutes before serving.

Chef's notes

  • Chilling the pasta first keeps the salad from going soggy in the bowl.
  • A teaspoon of olive oil in the dressing keeps the lemon bright and the pasta glossy.
